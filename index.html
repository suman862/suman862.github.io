About

I'm an AI/ML engineer and applied AI researcher with a background in physics. My journey from studying fundamental physical systems to exploring intelligent computational systems has shaped my approach to research: methodical, mathematical, and grounded in first principles.

Currently pursuing my Master's in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Madan Bhandari University of Science and Technology, I focus on understanding how AI systems can learn continuously, adapt efficiently, and represent information robustly. My research explores the intersection of computer vision, continual learning, and multimodal AI systems.

I'm interested in collaborative research that advances our understanding of learning mechanisms while building practical systems that solve real problems. My work balances theoretical depth with engineering rigor—I believe the best AI research is both scientifically sound and practically implementable.

Current Thesis

Continual Panoptic Segmentation with Visual Memory Bank

Investigating how AI systems can learn new visual concepts over time without forgetting previously learned knowledge. This research addresses the stability-plasticity dilemma in continual learning by exploring memory-based approaches for incremental panoptic segmentation.
