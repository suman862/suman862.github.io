01 / ABOUT

From Physics to Artificial Intelligence.

My academic journey began in Physics, where I developed an interest in mathematical modeling, scientific reasoning, and computational problem solving.

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Madan Bhandari University of Science and Technology (MBUST), Nepal. My coursework is complete, and I am working on my master's thesis.

My current research focuses on continual panoptic segmentation using a Visual Memory Bank, exploring how visual memory can help models retain knowledge while learning new categories.

Alongside research, I enjoy engineering practical AI systems, particularly multimodal inference pipelines, model serving, and scalable AI applications.
